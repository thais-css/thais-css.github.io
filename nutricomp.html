NutriComp

A small mobile app for comparing two grocery products using the priorities you set.

Open the live prototype ↗

The product question

Reading labels in a supermarket takes time, and there is no single “better” product. Protein, sugar, sodium, calories, fibre and fat matter differently depending on the person and the moment.

NutriComp keeps the choice simple: pick your priorities, scan two products and compare them.

Product flow

How it works

01Choose priorities

Select up to three goals. The first one matters most in the comparison.

02Scan two products

Use the camera to read their barcodes and retrieve available product data from Open Food Facts.

03Compare them

See which product matches each priority and why.

What I built

Mobile

Installable PWA

A mobile-first app with a manifest and service worker, so it can be installed and reused.

Scanning

Camera with fallback

It uses BarcodeDetector when the browser supports it, and ZXing when it does not.

Data

Public product API

It gets product information from Open Food Facts and handles missing names, images and nutrition fields.

Logic

Priority-weighted comparison

The order of the selected goals determines how much each category counts.

Normalization

Comparable values

Nutrition values are compared per 100g or 100ml, including fallbacks for calories and sodium.

Feedback

Clear results

Product cards show category winners and the differences behind the result.

What the result means

The result shows which of the two products is a closer match for the priorities selected. It does not give dietary or medical advice.

InputUp to three ranked goals.
MethodTransparent category weights and per-100g values.
LimitResults depend on the completeness of the public product data.
