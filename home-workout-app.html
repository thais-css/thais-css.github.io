Home workout app

A mobile app for planning, running and recording strength workouts at home, with the data kept on the device.

The problem I wanted to solve

A workout plan is only useful if it is easy to use mid-session. It needs to show what comes next, make it quick to record a set and keep enough history to make the next workout less of a guess.

I built this as a small, local-first app rather than a general fitness platform. It is designed around the parts of a home workout that are easy to lose track of when everything is spread across notes, timers and memory.

What I built

Local-first

Data stays on the device

Sessions and history are stored in IndexedDB through Dexie, with no account or cloud service required.

Session state

Pause and resume

A session can be paused and resumed. If someone chooses another workout, completed work is kept as an interrupted session.

In-session support

Timers that stay with the workout

Rest and timed-side controls are part of the flow, with optional sound, vibration and screen wake lock support.

History

Details, not just totals

Each record keeps the workout, exercises, completed sets and the effort or safety feedback entered during the session.

Interface

Mobile PWA

The app is designed for a phone during a workout and can be installed for repeat use.

Stack

React and TypeScript

The application uses React, TypeScript, Vite and a local IndexedDB database.

Limits are part of the design

This is a personal tracking tool. It helps record a planned workout, but it does not prescribe loads, diagnose injuries or replace professional guidance.

No personal recordsThe public page contains no real session history, health information or workout values.
No automatic prescriptionRecorded effort can inform future review, but the app does not change a workout on its own.
Local by defaultThe first version does not require an account or a cloud sync service.
