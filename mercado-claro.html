Mercado Claro

A personal app for understanding grocery spending and comparing the price of the same products across different supermarkets.

The question behind it

Shopping across several supermarkets makes it hard to know where money is going and whether a product is really cheaper. Receipt names vary, package sizes vary and the lowest shelf price is not always the best comparison.

I built Mercado Claro to turn receipt files into a usable record: what was bought, where it was bought and how the price compares when the products are actually equivalent.

Product flow

From receipt to comparison

01Add a receipt

Upload a PDF and confirm the shop and purchase date.

02Review the items

Keep the original receipt line and correct anything that needs a clearer name, category or package size.

03Standardise products

Save rules and links so equivalent products can be grouped without losing the original information.

04Compare prices

Use prices per kilo, litre or unit to compare like with like.

What I built

Receipt parsing

PDF to editable items

The app reads a receipt PDF, identifies likely item lines and lets the user check them before saving.

Data quality

A reviewable catalogue

Product names, brands, variants, categories and package sizes can be corrected without replacing the original receipt text.

Comparison logic

Prices on the same basis

Package sizes are parsed and prices are normalised by kilo, litre or unit when the information is available.

Product links

Exact products and families

Products can be linked as the same exact item or as part of the same comparison family.

Application

Full product workflow

The interface covers overview, receipt upload, product review and product comparison in one place.

Stack

TypeScript and SQLite

The application uses TypeScript, a web interface and a SQLite data layer managed with Drizzle.

What stays private

The app is used for a household’s real purchases. This public page explains the product and its logic, but does not link to the app or show receipt files, spending history, real shops, dates or prices.
